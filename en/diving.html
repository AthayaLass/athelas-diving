SDI Instructor Qualifications

I am a certified SDI (Scuba Diving International) instructor, offering comprehensive diving education and guided excursions. I teach the following specialty courses:

  • First Aid - CPR, AED and O₂ Provider - Emergency response training for diving incidents
  • Computer Diver - Master dive computer use for safer, longer dives
  • Navigation - Underwater navigation using compass and natural references
  • Advanced Buoyancy Control - Fine-tune trim and buoyancy for minimal impact
  • Marine Ecosystem Awareness - Understand marine habitats and responsible diving
  • Night/Limited Vision - Explore the underwater world in low-light conditions
  • Research (Scientific diving) - Methods for conducting underwater research

International Certification Standards

SDI is a member of the WRSTC (World Recreational Scuba Training Council), which establishes global standards for recreational scuba diving training. These are the same international standards recognized by major diving organizations worldwide, including PADI, SSI, and others. This means your SDI certification is recognized globally and follows the same rigorous safety and training standards as other leading certification agencies.

All certifications issued through SDI meet WRSTC standards, ensuring that your diving credentials are internationally recognized and accepted at dive centers around the world. Whether you're diving in the Caribbean, the Red Sea, or the Pacific, your SDI certification will be recognized wherever you go.
